    How it works

    No scissors, no glue gun. Four steps from a name to a picture you can print or share.

    1. 1

      Type a name

      Up to 14 letters in one of ten chunky felt fonts. Long names shrink toward the tip.

    2. 2

      Pick your felt

      Choose a color, a pattern like gingham or ditsy floral, and a texture from soft felt to glitter.

    3. 3

      Sew on the extras

      Add a peeking animal, lace trim, a side ribbon, buttons, beads and stickers.

    4. 4

      Download it

      Save a PNG with a whimsy backdrop, or just the pennant on a clear background.

    Make one for someone you love

    A nursery door, a dorm room, a birthday table. Type their name and watch it get stitched up.
